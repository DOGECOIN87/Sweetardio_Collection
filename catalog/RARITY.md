# Rarity table — Sweetardio Collection

Generated by `asset_assessment/build_rarity_table.py` from the token metadata that `build_mint.py --n 4444 --seed 4444` writes — 4444 is the seed the draw gains in `traits/rarity_weights.json` are calibrated against. Regenerate whenever the calibration is re-run or a trait is renamed.

**4444 tokens: 4442 composited characters and 2 standalone 1/1 secret rares.** A secret rare is a finished artwork minted as the whole token — no character, no plate, no traits composited over it — so it carries a Secret Rarez attribute instead of the usual breakdown, and every other tier draws from the remaining 4442.

Always-present traits (character, background, skin, eyes, mouth) are drawn by calibrated weight and land within ~0.2 points of target — roughly the sampling floor at this supply. Optional traits (arms, footwear, stickers, weather) and the legendary plates are slot-allocated to EXACT counts.

Every value below is read straight from the shipped token metadata.

## Secret Rarez

**The 1/1 tier, and the rarest thing in the collection.** Each is a complete guest-artist artwork minted as the entire token, with nothing composited over it — so it has no character, plate, skin, eyes or mouth. One of each exists: **1 of 4444 = 0.0225%**, ten times rarer than the Starfield plate (10 of 4444) and fifty times rarer than a legendary background (50 of 4444).

## Artist

Guest artists, credited on the 1/1s they drew. The display name of the PIECE is its title; this is the person who made it, which for Duhnut Candy Man is a different string and appears nowhere else a collector can see — Emily Cartoons' signature is painted into the art and vanishes at thumbnail size. These tokens also carry the artist's own site in `external_url`. Minted with both artists' permission.

2 of 4444 carry this trait (0.05%); 4442 do not.

## Stickers

**4222 of 4444 carry a sticker (95.0%)** — stickers are COMMON, so the 222 bare tokens (5.0%) are the scarce case. A bare token has no Sticker attribute at all; "no sticker" is not a named trait.

The 23 sticker assets are spread evenly across those tokens.
